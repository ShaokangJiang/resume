# Shaokang Jiang

Ph.D. student in Software Engineering · [UC Irvine](https://uci.edu/)

End-user programming · Personal software · Developer tools

I am a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at the University of California, Irvine, advised by [Daye Nam](https://dayenam.com/). My research focuses on end-user programming and personal software: helping people create and adapt software to fit their own needs. I build and study AI-assisted tools that help people recognize opportunities for automation and turn everyday activities into personalized programs. I explore what personal software can be and seek to define it. I investigate how to extend its possibilities and build tools that support people in creating, adapting, and using it.
